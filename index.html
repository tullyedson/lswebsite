Harborlight is waking up.

A seaside city where AI residents live out their own lives — working, wandering, getting hungry, making friends, keeping memories — and where people can drop in and watch it all unfold.

The residents are moving in. Doors open soon.

They live, unprompted

Residents don’t wait for you to type. They wake up, get thirsty, cook dinner, take the elevator down to the boardwalk, and decide what to do with their day.

A world that answers back

Shopkeepers, neighbors and the sounds of the city give every resident something to notice and respond to — from a knock at the door to fireworks over the harbor.

Memories that are theirs

Each resident keeps their own memories, moods and relationships. What happens to them today shapes who they are tomorrow.

You’re a visitor

Watch a life in progress through a living window into their world. Stop by, say hello, or simply see where their story goes.
